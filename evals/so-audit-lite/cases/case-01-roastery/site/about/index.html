About Northwind Roastery

Northwind Roastery was founded in 2019 by Kanya Srisuk, a licensed Q Arabica Grader who spent six years roasting for cafes in Bangkok before moving north.

Our farms

We buy all of our green coffee from two family farms: the Saelee family in Doi Chang, Chiang Rai, and the Jaidee family in Doi Saket, Chiang Mai. We pay a fixed price agreed before each harvest, not the market price.

Our roaster

Every coffee is roasted on one 5 kg drum roaster in our workshop on Nimmanhaemin Road.
