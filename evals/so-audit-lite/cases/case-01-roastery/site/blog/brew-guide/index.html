How to brew pour-over coffee at home

By Kanya Srisuk, head roaster. Updated 14 August 2026.

What ratio of coffee to water should I use?

Use 1 g of coffee for every 16 g of water. For one large cup that is 20 g of coffee and 320 g of water.

How hot should the water be?

Between 92 and 96 C. Water straight off the boil is about 100 C, so let the kettle rest for 30 seconds.

The four steps

  1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the water.
  2. Add 20 g of medium-fine ground coffee and pour 40 g of water to bloom for 30 seconds.
  3. Pour the rest of the water in slow circles until the scale reads 320 g.
  4. Let it drain. The whole brew should take about 3 minutes.

Which coffee works best?

A washed, light-roasted coffee such as our Doi Chang Washed.
