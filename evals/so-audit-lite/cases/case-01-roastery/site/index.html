Specialty coffee roasted in Chiang Mai

Northwind Roastery roasts small batches of single-origin Thai coffee every Tuesday. We buy green beans directly from two farms, one in Doi Chang and one in Doi Saket, and ship roasted coffee anywhere in Thailand within 3 days of roasting.

What we roast

Three coffees are on the menu all year: a washed Doi Chang for filter, a natural Doi Saket for espresso, and a house blend of both. Each bag is 250 g and printed with its roast date.

Why roast dates matter

Roasted coffee tastes best between 7 and 21 days after roasting. That is why we roast to order once a week instead of keeping stock on a shelf.
